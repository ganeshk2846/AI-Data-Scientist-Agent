Complete Project Requirements Document
Conversational Data Scientist Agent – AI for Business Intelligence


The application should be workable with any database which will be connected to it dynamically ,



1. Project Scope and Objectives
1. Develop an intelligent Conversational Data Scientist Agent.
2. Enable users to interact with business databases using natural language.
3. Support descriptive, diagnostic, impact, predictive, scenario-based and prescriptive analytics.
4. Support data discovery and database exploration.
5. Understand complex business questions.
6. Decompose multi-part questions into smaller analytical tasks.
7. Automatically select the appropriate analytical engine.
8. Combine SQL queries and Python-based data science operations.
9. Generate meaningful business insights.
10. Generate charts and visualizations automatically.
11. Maintain conversation context across multiple interactions.
12. Provide business recommendations based on analytical results.
13. Support scalable database ingestion and future expansion.
14. Provide a complete conversational analytics experience rather than just a question-to-SQL interface.
2. Question Types and Analytical Requirements
The system must support the following seven categories.
2.1 Data Discovery
1. Identify available databases and datasets.
2. List available tables.
3. Retrieve table descriptions.
4. Retrieve column names and descriptions.
5. Identify relationships between tables.
6. Identify available business metrics and KPIs.
7. Search for tables using natural-language descriptions.
8. Understand synonyms of table names and columns.
9. Identify which tables contain specific business information.
10. Explain what type of information is available in the database.
Example questions:
- What data do you have?
- Which tables contain customer information?
- What sales-related data is available?
- Which table contains product details?
2.2 Descriptive Analytics – WHAT?
1. Retrieve business metrics from databases.
2. Calculate sum, average, count, minimum and maximum.
3. Filter records based on user conditions.
4. Group records by dimensions.
5. Rank records and categories.
6. Calculate historical trends.
7. Identify top-performing and low-performing products.
8. Compare performance across regions.
9. Generate business summaries.
10. Calculate percentage changes.
11. Generate tabular results.
12. Generate appropriate charts.
Example questions:
- What were Q3 sales by region?
- What is the total revenue in May?
- Show the top 10 SKUs by margin.
- What are the revenue spikes across all months?
Required engines: SQL, Text-to-SQL, Pandas and visualization.
2.3 Diagnostic Analytics – WHY?
1. Identify reasons behind revenue increases and decreases.
2. Identify factors contributing to business performance changes.
3. Perform drill-down analysis by dimensions.
4. Compare performance across products, customers and regions.
5. Calculate variance and percentage changes.
6. Perform variance decomposition.
7. Perform correlation analysis.
8. Perform statistical tests.
9. Identify important features influencing business outcomes.
10. Analyze potential causal relationships when supported by data.
11. Identify factors responsible for poor performance.
12. Explain possible reasons behind business anomalies.
Example questions:
- Why did revenue decrease in March?
- What caused the revenue drop?
- Why are certain stores performing poorly?
- Which factors contributed to the sales decline?
Required engines: SQL, Python, statistical analysis and Scikit-learn.
2.4 Impact Analytics – SO WHAT?
1. Determine whether a business change is statistically significant.
2. Calculate confidence levels.
3. Calculate confidence intervals.
4. Measure business impact in monetary values.
5. Measure business impact in percentages.
6. Compare results against baselines.
7. Compare performance across segments.
8. Detect significant anomalies.
9. Identify significant spikes and dips.
10. Quantify the impact of observed business changes.
Example questions:
- Is this revenue dip significant?
- How much did the revenue decline cost us?
- Which region has the largest negative impact?
- Is this month's performance significantly lower?
Required engines: Statistics, significance testing, confidence intervals and anomaly detection.
2.5 Predictive Analytics – WHAT NEXT?
1. Forecast future revenue.
2. Forecast future sales.
3. Forecast product demand.
4. Forecast inventory requirements.
5. Predict customer churn.
6. Predict future business performance.
7. Predict future KPI trends.
8. Accept forecasting horizons from natural-language questions.
9. Generate prediction intervals.
10. Evaluate models using backtesting.
11. Compare forecasting model performance.
12. Generate forecast charts and tables.
Example questions:
- What will our revenue be three months from now?
- Forecast next quarter's demand.
- Which customers are likely to churn?
- What will the sales trend look like over the next six months?
Required engines: ARIMA, ETS, Prophet, regression, classification and Scikit-learn.
2.6 Scenario Analytics – WHAT IF?
1. Accept hypothetical business scenarios.
2. Simulate product price changes.
3. Estimate the impact of marketing budget changes.
4. Estimate the impact of demand changes.
5. Simulate opening new stores.
6. Evaluate different business scenarios.
7. Perform sensitivity analysis.
8. Identify important business drivers.
9. Compare baseline results with simulated results.
10. Present the estimated impact of each scenario.
Example questions:
- What if we increase product prices by 5%?
- What happens if we open two new stores?
- What if marketing expenditure increases by 10%?
- What happens if demand decreases by 15%?
Required engines: Simulation, Monte Carlo, elasticity models, regression coefficients and sensitivity analysis.
2.7 Prescriptive Analytics – NOW WHAT?
1. Recommend customer segments to target.
2. Recommend optimal inventory reorder levels.
3. Recommend business actions.
4. Rank recommendations based on expected impact.
5. Explain trade-offs between recommendations.
6. Optimize business decisions using constraints.
7. Support customer segmentation.
8. Identify products requiring attention.
9. Recommend strategies to improve business performance.
10. Provide actionable recommendations based on analytical findings.
Example questions:
- Which customer segment should we target?
- What is the optimal reorder level?
- Which products should receive more marketing investment?
- What actions should we take to improve revenue?
Required engines: Optimization, Linear Programming, SciPy Optimize and K-means clustering.
